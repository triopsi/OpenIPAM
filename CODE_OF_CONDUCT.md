# Community conduct

OpenIPAM welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, assume good intent and give specific, constructive feedback. Discuss the work without attacking people.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact details on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to protect the community, and will handle reports with discretion.
